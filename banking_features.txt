Feature: AutoPay
Explanation: AutoPay automatically pays your recurring bills on the due date, so you don't have to remember them every month.
- Never miss a bill payment
- Avoid late payment charges
- Save time by eliminating manual payments
- Can be disabled anytime

Feature: SIP
Explanation: A SIP automatically invests a fixed amount of money every month into a mutual fund, helping you build savings over time.
- Builds long-term wealth
- Small monthly investments
- Disciplined investing
- Flexible amount and cancellation

Feature: Insurance AutoPay
Explanation: Insurance AutoPay automatically pays your insurance premiums before the due date, ensuring your policy stays active without manual effort.
- Never miss a premium payment
- Keeps your insurance policy active
- Saves time every month
- Can be cancelled anytime
