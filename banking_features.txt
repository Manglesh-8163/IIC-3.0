Feature: AutoPay

Simple Explanation:
AutoPay automatically pays your recurring bills on the due date, so you don't have to remember them every month.

Benefits:

* Never miss a bill payment
* Avoid late payment charges
* Save time by eliminating manual payments
* Can be disabled anytime

Eligibility:
Customers who make recurring manual bill payments.

Activation Requirement:
Explicit customer consent.

===

Feature: SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

Simple Explanation:
A SIP automatically invests a fixed amount of money every month into a mutual fund, helping you build savings over time.

Benefits:

* Builds long-term wealth
* Small monthly investments
* Disciplined investing
* Flexible amount and cancellation

Eligibility:
Customers with regular income and no active SIP.

Activation Requirement:
Explicit customer consent.

===

Feature: Insurance AutoPay

Simple Explanation:
Insurance AutoPay automatically pays your insurance premiums before the due date, ensuring your policy stays active without manual effort.

Benefits:

* Never miss a premium payment
* Keeps your insurance policy active
* Saves time every month
* Can be cancelled anytime

Eligibility:
Customers who manually pay recurring insurance premiums.

Activation Requirement:
Explicit customer consent.

===

Feature: UPI AutoPay

Simple Explanation:
UPI AutoPay lets you automatically pay approved recurring subscriptions such as OTT services, mobile recharge, or utility bills using UPI.

Benefits:

* No need to authorize every payment
* Secure recurring payments
* Easy to manage or cancel
* Works with many popular services

Eligibility:
Customers with UPI enabled and recurring subscription payments.

Activation Requirement:
Explicit customer consent.
